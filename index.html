House rules

  • Dinner reservations are firm. Be at the table by the time on the card.
  • Tables are booked at every bar and club. No line, no cover. Arrive together for Stillife, Le Velvet, La Voûte and Flyjin so the whole group gets seated at once.
  • Bring an ID. Passports work, and you will be asked.
  • Card works everywhere. Canadian dollars; a little cash for tips is plenty.
  • Pickleball counts. Losing team buys Friday pre-drinks.
  • Group chat is the source of truth. Times shift, the chat wins.
